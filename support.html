Support

Set up, back up, restore and renew.

Contact us any time: zain.ahamed99@gmail.com. Your provider can also reach you by phone or WhatsApp using the number they gave you.

Setting up a new phone

  1. Install the app and open it.
  2. Enter the activation code from your provider.
  3. Fill in your business profile, or restore from a backup if you have one (see below).

Activating

Type the activation code exactly as you were given it. The app needs an internet connection the first time it activates. If it says “waiting”, your provider has not approved the activation yet; try again a little later or contact us.

Backing up to Google Drive

  1. Open the app’s settings and find the Google Drive backup section.
  2. Tap to connect and choose your Google account.
  3. Allow the app to access its backup files when asked.
  4. Backups are saved in a folder called “NexBook Backups” in your Drive, as dated files.

Restoring on a new phone

  1. Install the app and activate it (see above).
  2. Choose to restore from backup, and sign in to the same Google account that holds your backups.
  3. Pick the backup you want from the list. Dates and times are shown for each one.
  4. Wait for the restore to finish. The app shows what was restored and then opens.

The licence belongs to the phone, not the backup. If you changed phones, ask us to reset your old device first, then activate the new phone with the same code.

Renewing your subscription

Pay by bank transfer as agreed with your provider and send us the transfer receipt. We record the payment and your new paid-until date reaches the app the next time it connects to the internet. Open the app’s licence screen and tap “Check now” to update straight away.

The app says it is locked

Your records are safe. The app locks when a payment is overdue, when it has not been able to check your subscription for a week, or when a new version is required. Connect to the internet and tap “Check now”. If it stays locked, contact us.

Removing access or deleting data

To stop the app using your Google Drive, disconnect it in the app’s settings or remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions. See the privacy policy for more.
