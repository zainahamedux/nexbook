NexBook Mobile

Stock, shipments and invoices for small import businesses, on your own phone.

What the app does

NexBook Mobile is an Android app for shops and small importers who want to keep their records in one place without a computer.

  • Record shipments and the goods you receive, and scan barcodes to add products quickly.
  • Keep a product list with stock, cost and selling price.
  • Make invoices, keep customer balances, and record payments you receive.
  • Enter supplier purchase bills with transport and other costs, and see the final cost of each product.
  • See what you owe your suppliers and when payments are due.

Your records stay on your phone

Everything you enter is stored on your phone. The provider of the app cannot see your stock, customers or sales.

Optional backup to your own Google Drive

You can connect your own Google account to back up your records and restore them on a new phone. The app saves its backup files in a folder called “NexBook Backups” in your Google Drive. It can only open files that it created itself, and you can disconnect at any time from the app’s settings.

Subscription

The app is licensed to each business. You receive an activation code from your provider, and your access continues while your subscription is paid. See the terms of service for the details.
